# Prompt and skill library (Appendix E)

The prompts, skill/runbook templates and rules files of Appendix E. Fill the places in angle brackets (<...>) with your own data.

> Warning: do not put real personal data, customer records or company secrets into these prompts. Work with column names, types and masked or synthetic examples, and use the AI tool your company has approved.

Every task prompt has five parts (Chapter 2.3): **Task** (what, in one measurable sentence), **Input** (the evidence the model looks at), **Output** (the format), **Rule** (what it must not do) and **Presentation** (how you will verify the result).
