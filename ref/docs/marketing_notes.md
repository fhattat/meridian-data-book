# Marketing team notes on loyalty and campaign files (synthetic)

- MEMBER_NO: loyalty card number. 999 is the test card used by store IT.
- MKT_OPT_IN: Y if the member agreed to marketing messages; segments may only use Y.
- JOIN_DATE: date the card was issued.
- mm_campaign_log: one row per campaign and day: CAMPAIGN_ID, CAMPAIGN_NAME, CHANNEL, START_DATE, END_DATE, DAY, SPEND_USD, IMPRESSIONS.
- mm_campaign_products: product codes of a campaign, typed by hand in one cell.
